# Problem Statement & User Persona
## Accuknox — Alert Triage Workflow

---

## Problem Statement

Security engineers managing cloud infrastructure deal with a 
constant stream of alerts every day. The real problem is not 
that alerts exist — it is that when something critical fires, 
the engineer has to jump between multiple tools and screens 
just to answer three basic questions:

What exactly happened? How bad is it? What do I do right now?

This design brings everything — context, severity, remediation 
steps, ownership, and resolution — into one focused workflow 
so engineers can move from alert to resolved as fast as possible.

---

## User Persona

**Arjun — Cloud Security Engineer, 4 years experience**

Arjun works at a mid-size SaaS company managing infrastructure 
across AWS and GCP. He inherited his cloud accounts, which means 
he regularly discovers misconfigurations that should have been 
flagged months ago.

His biggest frustrations:
- Opens an alert with no useful context about what triggered it
- Has to Google remediation steps because the tool does not help
- No clear way to assign ownership to a teammate
- No audit trail when something is resolved

Arjun does not need more alerts. He needs better alerts — 
with enough context baked in that he can act immediately.
